Benjamin Blumenthal

Research Fellow (FRS-FNRS) · ECARES, Université Libre de Bruxelles

I am an economist working in Political Economy, Game Theory, and Organizational Economics. My research studies electoral accountability, lobbying, and policymaking, combining formal theory with applied methods.

I am currently a Research Fellow (FRS-FNRS) at ECARES, Université Libre de Bruxelles. Before that, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the Chair of Political Economy and eDemocracy, ETH Zürich, and I received my Ph.D. in Economics from the Paris School of Economics / EHESS in 2023.
