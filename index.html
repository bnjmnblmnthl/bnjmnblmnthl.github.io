Benjamin Blumenthal

FNRS Research Fellow · ECARES, Université Libre de Bruxelles

I am an economist using game theory to study accountability, lobbying, delegation, and experimentation.

I am currently an FNRS Research Fellow at ECARES, Université Libre de Bruxelles. Before that, I was a postdoc at ETH Zürich (D-MTEC), and I received my Ph.D. in Economics from the Paris School of Economics & EHESS in 2023.
